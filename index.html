Who is Dr. Glover?

Dr. Tom Glover has dedicated his professional life to serving the communities of Chula Vista and the South Bay. Starting as a teacher at Chula Vista Middle, he has served as an administrator at Eastlake High, Southwest High and Hilltop High before serving as the Principal of Eastlake Elementary and Castle Park High. He concluded his career by serving as an Executive level leader in the Sweetwater District as the Assistant Superintendent of Human Resources. These experiences give him the ability to appreciate a variety of perspectives from those who are working to better serve students and families.

He and his wife Monica have also sent their two children through Chula Vista schools, and they understand the needs of working parents who rely on the schools to support their values and ambitions for their children's futures.

He is eager to continue his service by providing oversight and direction as a member of the Chula Vista Elementary School Board Seat #5.

Community Matters

Besides his career in school, Dr. Glover has served on the Citizen Bonds Oversight Committee in the Sweetwater Union High School District and reviews schools across San Diego County as part of the Williams team visits.
